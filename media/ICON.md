# DotPilot icon / Ícono de DotPilot

## 0.6.1

Native vector refinement of the earlier DotPilot navigation/play mark and dot. The two flat blue facets share a horizontal seam, balanced proportions and subtly rounded outer corners. No gradients, shadows or decorative effects.

Refinamiento vectorial del símbolo anterior de navegación/ejecución y su punto. Dos planos azules, proporciones equilibradas y esquinas suavizadas. Sin degradados, sombras ni adornos.

- `dotpilot-icon.svg`: editable master, 128 × 128; charcoal tile `#181b20`, blue `#3794ff` and light blue `#75beff`.
- `dotpilot-icon.png`: optimized 128 × 128 raster export for VS Code's extension listing and DotPilot panel.
- `dotpilot-toolbar.svg`: the same paths without the background, framed for the editor toolbar.

The 0.6.1 artwork was edited directly as SVG and rasterized with Sharp. The earlier raster concept was generated with ImageGen. This is DotPilot's own identity, created for Camilo Ruiz · acrocode.dev; it is not a Microsoft logo.
